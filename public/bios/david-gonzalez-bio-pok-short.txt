DAVID GONZÁLEZ — POK SHORT BIO
POK (Proof of Knowledge) Country Manager, Colombia · Founder of merahki.ai

David González is POK's (Proof of Knowledge) Country Manager for Colombia — the Skills Intelligence infrastructure connecting education, skills, and work: more than 1,100 institutions across 20 countries use it to turn their credentials into data and intelligence about what they teach, the skills they develop, and the career outcomes they produce.

From Bogotá he leads its adoption across Colombia's leading universities, the cajas de compensación (employer-funded welfare and training funds), and the public sector. He is the founder of merahki.ai, where he has co-designed, virtualized, and run more than 20,000 hours of online learning for organizations such as AstraZeneca, Novartis, the Inter-American Development Bank, Claro, and Universidad de los Andes, across seven Latin American countries.
