DAVID GONZÁLEZ — SHORT BIO
Founder of merahki.ai · POK (Proof of Knowledge) Country Manager, Colombia

David González is the founder of merahki.ai, where he designs, produces, and measures micro-credentials for universities and enterprises with a data-driven approach, and POK's Country Manager for Colombia — the verifiable credentials platform used by more than 1,100 institutions across 20 countries, whose adoption he leads across Colombia's top universities, cajas de compensación, and public sector. Since 2018 he has produced more than 20,000 hours of online learning for organizations including Novartis, the Inter-American Development Bank, and Universidad de los Andes.
